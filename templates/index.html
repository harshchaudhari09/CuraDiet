01 — How it works

From a blood test to a plate, in three steps.

01

Your profile

Age, sex, height and weight, activity, smoking history — plus HbA1c, blood glucose and blood pressure from a recent check-up.

02

Risk = model + clinical rules

A HistGradientBoosting model estimates the probability of diabetes. Official ADA cut-offs for HbA1c and glucose sit on top as a safety layer — they can raise your risk level, never lower it.

03

A plan for your day

Calories from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, macros and limits from ADA and DASH guidance, split across your meals — with foods to eat more of and foods to limit.
